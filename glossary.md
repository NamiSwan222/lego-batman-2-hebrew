# Glossary: LEGO Batman 2: DC Super Heroes (Hebrew)

Family game, funny and light. Natural Hebrew like a kids' TV dub. Instructions to the player: masculine singular
imperative. Cutscene subtitles are "Speaker: line": keep the speaker name (translated per this list) and the colon.

## Markup rules

- Keep exactly: `[[SPECIAL]]`, `[[ACTION]]`, `[[JUMP]]`, `[[TAG]]` and every other `[[...]]` button code,
  `%s` `%d` `%i`, colour codes `~1`…`~9` and `~~` (put them around the matching Hebrew words, in the same
  order), and real line breaks.
- Write Hebrew in logical order; the build converts to the game's left-to-right drawing.
- Brand names LEGO / DC / WB stay Latin. No niqqud.
